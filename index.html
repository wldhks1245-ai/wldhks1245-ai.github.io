마이크로ZOOM 업로더

작은 뉴스도 크게 본다. 마이크로ZOOM 채널 운영자가 직접 만든 뉴스 쇼츠를 본인의 TikTok 임시저장함(드래프트)으로 보내는 개인용 도구입니다.

How it works

  • The operator signs in with their own TikTok account (Login Kit, user.info.basic).
  • When a video is ready, the operator approves it, and the app sends it to the operator's TikTok drafts inbox (Content Posting API, video.upload).
  • The operator finishes and posts the draft inside the TikTok app. The app never posts publicly by itself.
